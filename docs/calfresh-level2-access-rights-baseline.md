# The implementation started after the latest operations data

California's expanded CalFresh work and community-engagement rules began June
1, 2026. The official dashboard's latest operational month is May and its
latest error month is February. Those measures are a preimplementation
comparison surface, not candidate outcomes.

| Independent measure | Official value | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| CalFresh households, May 2026 | 3,107,208 | participation, not eligibility or take-up |
| CalFresh people, May 2026 | 5,166,532 | not the exposed ABAWD cohort |
| Applications received | 161,045 | monthly flow, not a closed cohort |
| Applications denied | 60,469 | not automatically wrongful |
| Procedural denials | 43,910 | 72.61% of dashboard denials; reason requires review |
| Thirty-day timeliness | 98.78% | speed does not establish correct access |
| Expedited timeliness | 96.52% | speed does not establish adequacy |
| February negative error rate | 26.08% | preimplementation quality measure |
| February active error rate | 10.80% | preimplementation quality measure |

The dashboard's last statewide churn series is 2020 Q4: 11.05% within 30 days
and 25.79% within 90 days. It is too stale to evaluate the 2026 mechanism.

The FY2025-26 hearings report records 44,504 CalFresh appeals filed, 32,769
withdrawn, 42,368 hearings scheduled, 10,434 held, 4,204 postponed, and 5,569
nonappearances. These are separate flows, not one denominator, and the report
does not identify H.R. 1 or ABAWD disputes. No success, failure, or rate may be
inferred by dividing them.

## Level 2 decision

The comparison baseline is complete. Candidate Level 2 is held pending at
least one full recertification cycle with candidate-coded exposure, exemption,
waiver, timeliness, churn, denial-correction, notice, hearing, language,
disability, household-adequacy, county-workload, and implementation-cost data.
