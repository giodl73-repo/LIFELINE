# LIFELINE context

LIFELINE is the Income Security and Family 2.0 domain owner for Taxlane's ISF
track. It measures whether fragmented benefits preserve household resources as
earnings change while protecting notice, appeal, adequacy, and distribution.

Current flagship: a deterministic aggregate benefit-continuity screen that
finds resource cliffs and take-up gaps. It is research tooling, not an
eligibility calculator, benefit determination, savings claim, or policy action.
