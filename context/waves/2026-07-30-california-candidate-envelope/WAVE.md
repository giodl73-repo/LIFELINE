# California CalFresh candidate-envelope wave

Outcome: make the enacted H.R. 1 implementation burden, proposed state fiscal
path, and household access exposure visible in one replayable record without
turning caseload contraction or lost benefits into efficiency.

## Delivered

- A dedicated `lifeline-calfresh` feature crate validates official fiscal-year
  rows and reconciles federal, state, and county shares.
- `candidate-baseline` exposes $86.8 million gross administration, a -$78.6
  million caseload-related administration offset, and $8.2 million net cost.
- `candidate-held-pack` supplies a bounded ISF candidate with its current-law
  mechanism, delivery owners, affected population, annual fiscal path, and
  explicit non-additivity rules.
- The pack holds savings and recommendation authority because observed access,
  rights, delivery, adequacy, and overlap results do not yet exist.

Level 1 candidate-envelope acceptance is complete. Level 2 admissibility is
held pending the closure conditions in
[candidate-role-review.md](reviews/candidate-role-review.md).
